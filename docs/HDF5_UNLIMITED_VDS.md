# Source-bound unlimited HDF5 mappings

Non-printf unlimited virtual mappings freeze their frame-axis selections against explicitly bound, fully allocated source datasets. Original unlimited selections and the finite effective source/virtual selections are separately exposed. The source dtype, stored axes and exact integer words remain authoritative. Native HDF5 decodes the concrete source datasets; its VDS source discovery and missing-source fill behavior are not used.

`tests/data/vds-unlimited` has six accepted cases with 27 frames: contiguous source exhaustion, temporal and spatial offsets/strides with a partial final block, source spatial stride, virtual spatial stride with explicit declared fill, and unequal-length alternating mappings that cover five frames. Expected values are generated by enumerating authored source coordinates and pairing them with virtual coordinates in HDF5 iteration order. The fixtures use compressed uint64 sources, including `u64::MAX` and words above `2^63`. Reverse and repeated native reads match every word.

A seventh case has two infinite contiguous mappings: one concrete source ends a frame before the other. The original shorter mapping still owns target coordinates within the combined output domain. Native admission rejects it, including with `UnmappedRegions::DeclaredFill`, rather than substituting missing source samples. Unchanged reference HDF5/FabIO reads supply declared fill in those positions; the proof records this policy difference separately from accepted count comparisons.

Producer restrictions are also separate from reader gaps. Both sides of an infinite source/virtual mapping must have the same selected cardinality in their non-unlimited dimensions in the tested HDF5 versions. A temporal reshape from two source frames of three samples to three virtual frames of two samples is rejected by `H5Pset_virtual`; it is not included as a valid unread native fixture. A partial-plane variant with unequal spatial cardinality is likewise rejected. Native HDF5 2.2.0 and reference HDF5 1.14.6 controls reject union/XOR/NOTB of an infinite hyperslab with an off-pattern finite block, and reject union with another infinite hyperslab. AND produces a finite selection (empty for the tested disjoint block). These controls do not imply that ordinary finite irregular selections are unsupported.

The reader retains all bound sources and selection declarations. Replacing or growing an inactive source invalidates even a read from another source, and failed reads clear visible output. Shapes, selected cardinalities, source/domain exhaustion and the existing resource budgets remain checked. Existing graph tests separately cover recursive finite composition and source-link policies.

The authored fixtures and expected coordinate/word arrays are CC0-1.0. No decoder is vendored; existing HDF5/native codec notices apply. The reference helper invokes unchanged pinned FabIO/h5py in fresh processes, checks every available native word and both original/effective selections, and fingerprints sources, imports, native library references and executable before and after.
